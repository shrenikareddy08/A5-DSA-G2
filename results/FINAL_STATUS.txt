IMPORTANT DESIGN CLARIFICATION
-------------------------------
HEFT V3 is the scheduling engine inside the project. The project
problem is intelligent scheduling of real text-processing workloads,
not demonstrating HEFT in isolation.

Text algorithms are the workload.
The document store supplies real input.
The scheduler decides when and where tasks execute.
The runtime learner improves future predictions.
